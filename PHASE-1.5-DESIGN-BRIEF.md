# Product Design brief — SHORE DASH

### Experience goal
Make mobile play feel immediate and unobstructed: the canvas is the controller, gestures are the interface, and the beach path is the thing the player reads and reacts to.

### Interaction model
- Tap: jump.
- Swipe up: jump.
- Swipe left/right: quick directional dodge.
- Swipe down: slide.

### Visual hierarchy
HUD stays in screen space and remains readable in portrait and landscape. No permanent virtual joystick or action-button chrome covers the gameplay.

### Runner structure
Use one continuous path rather than three lanes. The path can drift in center and width, so positioning remains meaningful without lane snapping.

### Product rule
Borrow proven runner usability patterns, but keep SHORE DASH's identity in its beach, surf, wave, and path-change mechanics rather than copying another game's assets or branding.
